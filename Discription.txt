Online Shopping
Online shopping is the process of buying goods or services over the internet. It allows consumers to browse, select, and purchase products from anywhere, at any time, using digital devices like computers, smartphones, or tablets.

Benefits of Online Shopping
- *Convenience*: Shop from anywhere, 24/7.
- *Wide Selection*: Access to a vast range of products and services.
- *Price Comparison*: Easily compare prices across different sellers.
- *Time-Saving*: Avoid physical store visits and long queues.
- *Accessibility*: Shop from the comfort of your own space.

Popular Online Shopping Platforms
- *E-commerce Websites*: Amazon, Flipkart, Myntra.
- *Marketplaces*: eBay, Etsy.
- *Brand Websites*: Nike, Apple.

Tips for Safe Online Shopping
- *Verify Website Authenticity*: Ensure the website is legitimate and secure.
- *Check Reviews*: Read reviews from other customers.
- *Use Secure Payment Methods*: Opt for trusted payment gateways.
- *Keep Personal Info Safe*: Be cautious with sensitive information.

Common Online Shopping Categories
- *Fashion*: Clothing, accessories, footwear.
- *Electronics*: Smartphones, laptops, gadgets.
- *Home and Kitchen*: Furniture, appliances, decor.
- *Beauty and Personal Care*: Skincare, haircare, cosmetics.
